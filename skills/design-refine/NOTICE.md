# Notice

`critic.md` draws on two MIT-licensed skills for its structure and vocabulary. Its text is written in this repo's own words.

- `humbleteam/design-review` (MIT): the review and before/after comparison modes, Before/After/Why findings with one Nielsen or WCAG citation each, a capped ranked list with no minimum count.
- `Prasad-D-Ware/critiquing-visual-design` (MIT): the eight visual principles (contrast, hierarchy, alignment, proximity, repetition, balance, white space, unity) used as a critique checklist.

The accessibility pass is written from WCAG 2.1 AA itself.

No text from `grapeot/design_skill` is included; it has no licence. The Intent/Execution/Preference filter is an idea it shares, reimplemented here in our own words.
